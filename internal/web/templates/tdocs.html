A TDoc number is looked up through its group's meeting list (DynaReport) and downloaded from the meeting's Docs/ folder. Name the meeting only when a number cannot be found on its own.

A document without a TDoc number, such as a meeting report, can be opened by its path under https://www.3gpp.org/ftp/, for example tsg_ran/WG1_RL1/TSGR1_123/Report/Final_Minutes_report_RAN1#123_v100.zip.
